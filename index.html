System

From floorplan to buildable interior

The product thesis is simple: the room should be structured before it is visualized. The page below stays at the high level on purpose and avoids leaking unnecessary pipeline internals.

Step 01
Layout

Generate a furnished room layout that makes sense for the actual apartment and intended use.

Step 02
Validity

Check that the room is usable, coherent, and not just visually plausible.

Step 03
Matching

Connect the concept to real furniture and materials while preserving style, dimensions, and budget logic.

Step 04
Rendering

Produce the final interior image only after the room concept is grounded in real-world constraints.

Product thesis

The output is not just a render. It is a room concept that can move toward implementation.

Real items

Furniture and materials can be tied to real catalog items instead of made-up objects that only work in the image.

Real constraints

Dimensions, layout coherence, and budget logic can survive the generation process instead of getting lost inside a pretty render.

Real workflows

The result can feed furnishing, sourcing, and renovation workflows rather than ending as marketing-only creative.

Moat

The hard part is not image generation. The hard part is making generated interiors real.

Image generation is becoming cheap and accessible. The harder thing is producing rooms that are structurally plausible, style-consistent, and grounded in real products and downstream execution.

What the moat is
  • layout intelligence grounded in real room logic
  • validity checks for usable planning
  • compatibility logic across products and constraints
  • catalog grounding instead of fake furniture
  • controlled generation instead of free hallucination
What the moat is not
  • not one prompt
  • not one diffusion model
  • not one impressive render
  • not generic “AI interior design” positioning
  • the moat is the full system that connects generation to implementation
Wedge

Start where the pain is clearest: empty apartments

Empty apartments are a clean starting wedge because they have a recurring need for presentation, furnishing direction, and faster handoff into move-in readiness. This is the point where image generation alone stops being enough.

Why this starting point works

  • clear visual before/after value
  • repeatable input format
  • direct link to furnishing and renovation decisions

Who can feel the pain first

  • developers
  • brokers and leasing teams
  • furnishing and renovation flows around move-in

Where it can expand

  • furnishing packages
  • sourcing workflows
  • renovation planning
  • transaction layer around implementation
